Our story

A Milpitas classic, with a new family at the stove

For more than thirty years, Chez Christina was the place on Jacklin Road for a quiet dinner and a good steak. In August 2026, owner Apple and her family took over the kitchen and reopened it as Chez Christina by Casa Tala.

Tala means “star” in Filipino. We cook Filipino favorites with Asian and international touches, and we serve them with real Filipino hospitality: generous plates, a warm welcome and a table that always has room for one more.

Come as guests, leave as family.
