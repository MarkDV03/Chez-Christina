Our story

A taste of home & beyond

Chez Christina by Casa Tala proudly continues a 30-year legacy of serving good food and bringing people together. For three decades, Chez Christina was the place on Jacklin Road for a quiet dinner and a good steak.

In this new chapter, owner Apple and her family are introducing Filipino–Asian fusion. Our mission is to serve delicious food, so every gathering becomes a cherished memory. Tala means “star” in Filipino, and we hope you'll make our table your own.

Come as guests, leave as family.
